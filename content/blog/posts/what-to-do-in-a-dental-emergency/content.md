Dental emergencies rarely happen at a convenient time. Knowing a few first steps can protect your smile and reduce pain until you can get professional care in St. Petersburg.

## Severe toothache

Rinse gently with warm water, floss to remove trapped food, and use a cold compress on your cheek for swelling. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gums. Call us if pain is intense, lasting, or paired with fever.

## Chipped or cracked tooth

Save any broken pieces if possible, rinse your mouth with warm water, and cover sharp edges with dental wax or sugar-free gum temporarily. A cold compress can help with swelling until your appointment.

## Knocked-out tooth

Hold the tooth by the crown (not the root), rinse briefly if dirty, and try to place it back in the socket. If that is not possible, keep it in milk or saliva and seek care immediately — timing matters for saving the tooth.
